🧩 Add Scooby to Chrome

Install in under a minute. Download the extension, load it into Chrome, and you're ready.

1

Download the zip

Click the button above to download scooby-chrome-extension.zip and unzip it.

2

Open Extensions

In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.

3

Load unpacked

Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped chrome-extension folder.

4

Pin & use

Click the 🧩 puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar, pin Scooby, and click it any time to summarize.

  2. 2Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
